What Evidence Is Useful?

Arena Rules Center

1. Match & Gameplay Rules

Players must join the custom room at least 5 minutes before match start. Emulators and PC players are strictly barred unless designated as PC lobby. Teaming up with opponents results in immediate elimination and forfeit of entry.

2. Financial & Payment Policy

1 Arena Coin = ₹1. All coin transactions via Khalti and eSewa are manually verified by verified arbiters. Duplicate or forged transaction IDs trigger an instant 14-day ban.

3. Malicious Report Penalty

To protect legitimate players, filing false reports with malicious intent or doctored screenshots incurs a strict 20 Coin penalty deducted from the reporter's wallet.

4. Fair Play & Dispute Resolution

All score disputes require full screenshot or video evidence uploaded within 15 minutes of match conclusion. Arbiter decisions are logged to the immutable audit ledger.
